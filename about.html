Organization History

UNIQ-CUTZ was established in 1999 in the front room of my grandmother's house. My grandma, Magriet de Koker, was well known for her passion of cutting hair and her broad knowledge of hair maintenance. It started small with family members cutting their hair to neighbours coming and not long, the whole town was abuzz.

My mother used to help her with washing the towels and keeping the room clean. My mother then went to study further on hairstyling and she assisted her mother after studying. This way it assisted their clientele to only get bigger and better with every new technique. There was challenges but the two of them, overcame each challenges by sticking together and strategizing. My granny, now retired, still uses the salon services for her own hair. I now shadow my mother and i intend to take the family legacy to the next level.

Organization Vision Statement

Our vision is to become the go-to destination for all hairt types, giving personalized and sustainable hair care solutions. We aim to create a welcoming and relaxing environment where our clients can feel pampered and leave feeling radiant.

Organization Mission Statement

Our mission is to be innovative and motivated in the pursuit of excellence and embrace the challenges of change in the hair industry. To strive for perfection in UNIQ-CUTZ training programs, so that our hair stylists can contribute to the hair industry for the benefit of our clientele.
